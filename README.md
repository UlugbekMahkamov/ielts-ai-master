## 🎯 Reading & Listening Skills (Article & Podcast)

Har bir artikl/podcast uchun AI avtomatik yaratadi:

### ❓ Comprehension Savollar (20+ ta)
- **True/False/Not Given** (5 ta)
- **Multiple Choice** (5 ta) - 4 options A/B/C/D
- **Summary Completion** (5 ta)
- **Sentence Completion** (3 ta)
- **Short Answer** (2 ta)

Har bir savol uchun:
- ✅ To'g'ri javob
- 💡 Uzbekcha tushuntirish
- 📊 Qiyinlik darajasi (1-5)

### 🔍 Matn Tahlili
- **Vocabulary Tier Map**: B1/B2/C1/Band 7+ so'zlar
- **Collocations**: Tabiiy so'z birikmalari
- **Sentence Patterns**: Band 7+ gap qurilmalari

## 🗣️ Speaking Assessment (4 IELTS Mezon)

Har bir speaking javobingiz AI tomonidan baholanadi:

| Mezon | Tavsif |
|-------|--------|
| **Fluency & Coherence** | Ravonlik, pauzalar, mantiqiy bog'lanish |
| **Lexical Resource** | So'z boyligi, paraphrasing, idiomatik ifodalar |
| **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** | Gap tuzilmalari xilma-xilligi, xatolar |
| **Pronunciation** | Stress, intonation, connected speech, individual sounds |

### Qo'shimcha Xususiyatlar:
- 🔧 **Line-by-line corrections**: Original → Corrected → Explanation (uzbekcha)
- 🎯 **Uzbek-accent-specific tips**: /θ/, /ð/ tovushlari, word stress, IPA
- 📚 **New vocabulary suggestions**: Band 7+ so'zlar misollar bilan
- ⬆️ **Improvement actions**: Keyingi band uchun 3 ta aniq qadam
